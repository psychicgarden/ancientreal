    uint256 principal,
    uint256 rate,
    uint256 periods

        result = (result * (PRECISION + rate)) / PRECISION;

}

**"If we have $10 million in our lending pool earning 10% annually, that's $1 million in yield. We keep $200,000."**

**"When investors want to exit, they sell their property shares on our secondary marketplace. We take 2% of every transaction."**

**"Family offices and property developers pay us to deploy our infrastructure for their own property portfolios. Think 'Stripe for real estate tokenization.'"**

## ❓ HANDLING COMMON QUESTIONS (Prepare These Answers)

### Q: "What if regulations change and tokenized real estate becomes illegal?"

**A:** "That's moving in the opposite direction. The SEC just approved Bitcoin ETFs. Switzerland, Singapore, and UAE have clear legal frameworks for tokenized securities. Traditional finance is adopting blockchain, not fighting it. The regulatory risk is declining, not increasing."

---

### Q: "What about property management? Who handles tenants, maintenance, etc.?"

**A:** "We partner with local property management firms in each market. They handle operations. We handle the capital stack and blockchain infrastructure. Think of us as the technology layer, not the operator. Stripe doesn't run e-commerce stores - they process payments. We don't manage properties - we tokenize ownership."

---

### Q: "How do you compete with Airbnb or Vrbo for rental income?"

**A:** "We don't compete with them - we USE them. Most of our properties are listed on Airbnb and generate short-term rental income. We're the ownership layer, not the booking platform. It's complementary, not competitive."

---

### Q: "What happens if property values crash?"

**A:** "Two things: First, we're targeting high-growth markets with strong fundamentals (coastal properties, digital nomad hubs, emerging markets). Second, even if property values decline 20%, investors are still cash-flow positive from rental income. Traditional mortgages default when values drop. Ours don't - because the yield is independent of valuation."

---

### Q: "Why Avalanche? Why not Ethereum?"

**A:** "We started on Avalanche testnet for speed and cost. But our contracts are EVM-compatible - they'll deploy to Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, and any EVM chain. We're chain-agnostic infrastructure. Think cross-chain from day one."

---

### Q: "How do you prevent money laundering or fraud?"

**A:** "Every investor goes through KYC/AML verification before they can purchase property shares. We integrate with Sumsub and Jumio for identity verification. We have the same compliance standards as Coinbase or Kraken. This isn't DeFi summer 2020 - this is institutional-grade compliance."

---

### Q: "What's your cap table look like? Who else is invested?"

**A:** *(Be honest - if you're pre-seed, say so)*  
**"We're pre-seed right now. Founders own 80%, with 20% reserved for this round and an employee option pool. We've been building for 18 months on our own capital to prove the tech works before raising. We're not a pitch deck - we're a product."**

---

### Q: "Why are you raising now instead of 6 months from now when you have more traction?"

**A:** "Because we're at the inflection point. The tech is proven. The market is exploding. The next 12 months will determine who owns this space. We can bootstrap to 100 properties and be a small player, or we can raise capital now, deploy aggressively, and be the category leader. Speed is everything in a market moving this fast."

---

### Q: "What's your exit strategy?"

**A:** "Three paths: One, we become the Stripe of tokenized real estate and go public in 5-7 years. Two, we get acquired by a major prop-tech player like Zillow, Redfin, or Compass who wants blockchain infrastructure. Three, we become a cash-flow positive infrastructure business that never needs to exit. All three paths return 10x+ to early investors."

## 🏆 FINAL TIPS FOR DELIVERY

### Tone & Energy:
- **Confident, not cocky**
- **Data-driven, not salesy**
- **Visionary, not delusional**

### Pacing:
- **Slow down on key numbers** (valuation, revenue, return projections)
- **Speed up on technical details** (don't bore them with code)
- **Pause after big statements** (let them absorb)

### Body Language:
- **Open posture** (no crossed arms)
- **Direct eye contact** (builds trust)
- **Controlled hand gestures** (emphasize key points)
- **Lean forward when listening** (shows engagement)
- **Lean back when closing** (shows confidence)

### What to Wear:
- **If pitching to crypto VCs:** Hoodie and jeans (you're a builder)
- **If pitching to traditional VCs:** Business casual (you're serious)
- **If pitching to family offices:** Suit, no tie (you're professional but modern)

### The #1 Rule:
**Act like you've already won. Because you have.**

The product is built. The code is audited. The market is exploding. You're not asking for a favor - you're offering them a chance to be part of something inevitable.
